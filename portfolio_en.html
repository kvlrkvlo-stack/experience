Performance Review, assessment, career progression and employee development existed as disconnected activities. They needed to be connected into a single transparent system, where assessment outcomes clearly lead to decisions on development, promotion and reward.

  • Mapped the existing review process and the points where transparency was lost.
  • Gathered managers' and directors' expectations for assessment, promotion and calibration.
  • Identified the missing links: assessment → grade → development → compensation.
  • Redesigned Performance Review.
  • Developed grading architecture.
  • Created promotion criteria.
  • Connected grading to 360° and Performance Review.
  • Introduced 9-box, HiPo and HiPro.
  • Connected outcomes to IDPs, PIPs, promotions, responsibility scope and compensation decisions.
  • Trained managers in calibration, feedback and career conversations.

A single cycle: an employee completes Performance Review and 360°, results map onto the grading grid and 9-box, and from there concrete decisions emerge - IDPs for development, PIPs when underperforming, calibration of compensation and responsibility scope, HiPo/HiPro candidates. Managers worked within a shared logic thanks to calibration and feedback training.

360° Assessment & HR Automation

One assessment process, automated from questionnaire to result

360° assessment was used widely - across Performance Reviews, career tracks, competency assessment, development planning and probation feedback. But administration was almost entirely manual and consumed cycle time.

  • Ran an assessment cycle end to end and logged the manual steps and time losses.
  • Isolated the recurring scenarios that could be standardized and automated.
  • Defined how results should connect to grading, reviews and development.
  • Formalized the process, roles and timelines.
  • Developed questionnaires and scales.
  • Created multiple assessment scenarios.
  • Connected results to grading, reviews and development.
  • Automated administration and result collection.

Launching assessments, reminders, collecting responses and generating results ran through a Bitrix24 + Jinn + iSpring + Pachca chatbot setup. Manual routine was replaced by a configured scenario, and results fed straight into development and reviews.

The goal was to build a consistent employee journey - from preboarding through probation to further development - so the new-hire experience was predictable and managers had clear checkpoints.

  • Collected the points where new hires lost momentum or context.
  • Aligned end-of-probation expectations with managers.
  • Defined the materials and practices needed at each stage.
  • Preboarding and onboarding.
  • Learning plans and probation monitoring.
  • End-of-probation 360°.
  • Competency matrices and IDPs.
  • Regular 1:1s and feedback practices.
  • Certification and learning system.

One employee journey: preboarding → day one → probation with checkpoints → wrap-up via 360° → development. Each stage has its own goal and clear tasks.
